Stories told under the banyan tree

Short animated stories from India for everyone: Panchatantra fables that end with their moral, the Ramayana and the Mahabharata told in short parts, and episodes about the characters who shaped them.

Watch on YouTube

Where the stories come from

  • FablesRetold from the Panchatantra, a public-domain collection of ancient Indian fables.
  • RamayanaBased on Valmiki's Ramayana in Ralph T. H. Griffith's public-domain English translation (1870s).
  • MahabharataBased on Kisari Mohan Ganguli's public-domain English translation (1883–1896).

We tell the stories respectfully and in our own words. All characters are our own original designs; we do not copy the look of any comic, film or television series.

How the videos are made

The videos are made with an automated pipeline: a script written from the sources above, a synthetic (AI) narration voice, AI-generated illustrations and short AI-animated clips in our own styles, and music. Every video is labelled as containing AI-generated content, on screen and in its description.
