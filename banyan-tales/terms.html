Stories and sources

Banyan Tales retells traditional stories in its own words: Panchatantra fables, and the Ramayana and Mahabharata based on public-domain English translations (Ralph T. H. Griffith's Ramayana, 1870s; Kisari Mohan Ganguli's Mahabharata, 1883–1896). Retellings are shortened for video, and harsh details in some fables are softened for a family audience. We aim to tell every story respectfully.

Characters and art

All characters are our own original designs. AI-generated illustrations and AI-animated clips are labelled as such on screen and in each video's description. We do not copy the look of any comic, film or television series. If you believe something is wrongly credited or too close to someone else's work, please open an issue and we will fix or remove it.
