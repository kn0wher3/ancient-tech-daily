How did they build that?

Short videos about ancient engineering: iron that barely rusts, stepwells, Roman concrete, clocks and canals. Each one tells how it was made, why, and why it still stands, using facts from cited sources.

Watch on YouTube

How we tell the facts

Every claim in a video carries a label, so you can see how sure it is:

  • Science saysA measured, dated or peer-reviewed finding.
  • Text saysAn ancient text, inscription or museum record states it.
  • InterpretationScholarly inference or open debate, always hedged.

No aliens, lost super-civilisations or "scientists are baffled". Sources are listed in every video description.

How the videos are made

The videos are produced with an automated pipeline: research from public sources (mainly Wikipedia), a script that is checked against those sources, a synthetic (AI) narration voice, our own drawn animations, and freely licensed images. Images are public domain, CC0 or CC BY with visible credit. Any AI-generated illustration is labelled on screen as "AI-generated illustration". Full image credits are in each video's description.
